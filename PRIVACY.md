# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** January 12, 2026

**WOD Gen Ultimate** ("we", "our", or "us") operates as a standalone, offline-first application. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our Service.

## 1. Information Collection and Use

**We do not collect any personal information.**

*   **Offline Operation**: The App runs entirely on your device. It does not communicate with external servers to generate workouts or process data.
*   **Local Storage**: Any data, such as your workout history, generated logs, or configuration preferences, is stored strictly in your device's local storage (`localStorage`). This data is never transmitted to us or any third parties.

## 2. Device Permissions

The App may request the following permissions for specific functionality:

*   **Wake Lock**: To keep the screen on during a workout.
*   **Storage/Photos**: Only if you choose to save or share a workout image locally (Android).

## 3. Third-Party Services

We do not use any third-party analytics, tracking, or advertising services.

## 4. Children's Privacy

Our Service does not address anyone under the age of 13, but since we do not collect data, there is no risk of unauthorized data collection from minors.

## 5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.

## 6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer.
